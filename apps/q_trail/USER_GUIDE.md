# `q_trail` — Investigator & Auditor User Guide

## 1. Investigative Workflow
1. **Define Source Entity:** Input the origin account or corporate entity under suspicion.
2. **Execute Flow Stitcher:** Q-Trail correlates multi-bank timestamps and identifies pass-through conduits where funds were moved within 24 hours.
3. **Analyze Circular Flows:** Highlight closed loops where funds flowed through intermediary shell entities back to the target director/promoter.
4. **Export Sankey Diagram:** Export publication-ready interactive fund flow diagrams for inclusion in charge-sheets and forensic dossiers.
