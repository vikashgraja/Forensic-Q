# `q_bank` — Investigator & Auditor User Guide

## 1. Investigative Workflow
1. **Ingest Statements:** Upload PDF/CSV bank statements under the Q-Bank Ingestion panel.
2. **Review Auto-Flagged Transactions:** Filter by `High Risk (>75)` or `Escalated` to review suspicious flows immediately.
3. **Inspect Pass-Throughs:** Examine rapid inflow-to-outflow wires matching shell company patterns.
4. **Export Findings:** Download the filtered ledger as CSV or print case-ready transaction dossiers.
