# `q_link` — Investigator & Auditor User Guide

## 1. Investigative Workflow
1. **Initiate Entity Search:** Search for any suspect person, company, PAN, or bank account.
2. **Expand Correlation Radius:** Discover 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-degree links across emails, transactions, and corporate registries.
3. **Isolate Shell Networks:** Highlight entities that share registered office addresses or nominal directors.
4. **Export Master Link Chart:** Generate high-resolution evidence relationship maps for legal filings.
